# Template industry pack

Tier: researched (per-clock tiers live in `clocks.yaml`)
Last reviewed: 2026-11-03
Owner: unassigned

Copy this folder to `skills/industry_packs/<slug>/`, keep every heading, and fill it from authoritative sources using the `pack_research` skill. Story material lives here; every deadline lives in `clocks.yaml`; scoring defaults live in `defaults.yaml`.

## Who this pack is for

One paragraph: the organizations this pack fits, their size range, and the regulators and standards bodies that matter to them.

## Typical scenarios

Three to five scenario families that are realistic for the sector, one line each on why. Keep the list defensive: it describes what the organization must respond to, not how to carry out an intrusion.

## Stakeholders and roles

Who sits at the table in this sector, using the sector's own titles.

## Hardest decisions

The three to five decisions that teams in this sector get wrong under pressure, each with the tension it creates.

## Notification clocks

Summary only; `clocks.yaml` is the source of truth. List each clock id, who it goes to, the deadline, and its tier.

## Exercise requirements

What regulation, standard, examiner expectation, or insurer requires the exercise and how often, with the citation (also recorded in `clocks.yaml` under `exercise_requirements`).

## Sector vocabulary

Terms the injects should use so the scenario sounds like the organization's own incident, not a generic one.

## Sources

Every URL used, one per line, with the date it was read.
